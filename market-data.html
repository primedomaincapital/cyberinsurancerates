Market data · Cyber insurance premiums, rate trends, and claim costs

Cyber cover got cheaper. Claims did not get simple.

After three years of punishing rate increases, cyber insurance pricing turned in 2025 — and kept turning. Premiums fell while ransomware demands climbed, a combination that looks contradictory until you separate price from loss: carriers are charging less per dollar of cover, but the claims that do arrive, especially extortion claims, are bigger and messier than before.

The flat policy count is the figure that should worry the industry more than any rate index. Cheaper cover mostly meant existing buyers paying less, not new buyers arriving — so the protection gap is unchanged while the threat keeps professionalizing. For a buyer, the practical read is narrow: price is negotiable again, but underwriters still discount documented controls, and the claims data rewards fast reporting and refused ransoms more than any single product feature.
